## Direct or through the data hub

QF2Odoo and QF2Oseon connect QuotationFactory directly to Odoo and Oseon respectively. The UNS integrations bring data into a shared data layer; a configured receiving application is still needed to process it. QF2UNS and Oseon2UNS do not automatically form a ready-made QuotationFactory-to-Oseon route.

<Note>
  The Odoo guides cover Odoo 19. Available screens and exchanges depend on your installed release and configuration.
</Note>
