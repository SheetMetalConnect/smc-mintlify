QF2Odoo imports a QF project into an Odoo quotation and its related engineering records. This page shows where to find the data and which system owns it.

[Nederlands](/nl/qf2odoo/imported-data)

## Quotation and customer

| From QuotationFactory | Where to find it in Odoo | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Buying company and contacts | Customer on the quotation; Contacts | The company must already exist. Supplied contacts are matched by email or created under it. The first supplied contact is the order contact. |
| Quoted items, quantities and unit prices | Quotation lines | The connector supplies QF lines and prices. Odoo owns taxes and financial processing. |
| Requested delivery date | Quotation commitment date | The estimated date is kept separately as source information; it does not replace the requested date. |
| Payment and delivery terms, quotation header and footer | Native terms where matched; **QF Quotation** tab for supplied source content | The connector links matching Odoo terms. Unsupported source terms remain visible on the QF tab. |
| Drawings and source export | Quotation attachments and related records | The original export is attached to the quotation; access to its source information is for internal users. |
| QF project, status and revision | Quotation source information; optional **QuotationFactory** tab and **QF Projects** | Source status is informational. It does not confirm the order. |

## Parts, assemblies and purchased items

The connector creates engineering snapshots for manufactured work. The resulting products, bills of materials and operations are native Odoo records; the connector also adds QF source information and instructions. In the optional workspace, **QF BOMs** on the quotation opens the linked bills of materials. Without it, find them in standard Odoo Manufacturing.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Single manufactured part">
    A manufactured part becomes a revision-specific Odoo product with its bill of materials, material components and applicable operations. The material article links to an existing Odoo catalogue product.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Assembly and multilevel BOM">
    An assembly becomes a revision-specific product. Its native Odoo bill of materials contains the assembly components; nested assemblies retain their own levels and applicable operations.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Purchased item">
    An item with purchasing steps only links to an existing Odoo article. The connector does not create a manufacturing bill of materials for it or replace the catalogue article's routes.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

| From QuotationFactory | Where to find it in Odoo | Standard record or connector addition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Part or assembly identity, dimensions and supplied engineering details | Product and its QF source information | Native product plus QF fields and source information. Availability depends on supplied data. |
| Assembly structure, material and component quantities | Manufacturing → Bills of Materials | Native bill of materials and component lines. Existing catalogue materials remain Odoo-owned. |
| Ordered working steps and resource codes | Bill of materials → Operations and linked work centre | Native operations and work centres; supplied QF instructions and details are added. |
| Supplied project, product and operation comments | QF production instructions on relevant production and work orders | Connector-added instructions. Production and work orders arise through Odoo's normal rules after confirmation. |
| Explicit outsourced operation and supplier | Subcontracting bill of materials and supplier | Native Odoo subcontracting structure, when the QF resource explicitly identifies a supplier. |

## What must exist and when?

The required integration add-on and matching connector are needed for every import. The workspace is optional: it adds navigation and views, not the underlying native quotation and manufacturing records.

| When | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Import | The buying company must resolve to an existing Odoo customer. Existing materials and purchased articles must match uniquely; the connector does not create catalogue companies or those articles. |
| When applicable to the export | In-house QF resource codes must match Odoo work centres; outsourced resources need matching suppliers. A supplied currency must be active and resolve to an appropriate Odoo pricelist, or to the company-currency default where supported. |
| When synchronisation is enabled | QF requests the configured customer, supplier or article data from Odoo. These exchanges are optional and require the corresponding eligible master data. |
| Confirmation and production | The import must be complete. Staff confirm the quotation in Odoo after their commercial checks. Odoo's configured routes, procurement and manufacturing rules determine the resulting work. |

A project import creates the draft and engineering data, but no manufacturing or stock demand. A standalone product-only or BOM-only import is not a documented mode.

## Who owns a revision?

A changed export with a new reference creates a linked draft and its own engineering snapshots. Earlier quotations, manufacturing orders and their instructions remain intact. Shared catalogue articles stay under Odoo ownership. Shipping and manual preparation can be carried forward, while the new export supplies its own QF content. A verified identical replay returns the earlier result rather than creating another revision.
