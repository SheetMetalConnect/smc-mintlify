Before your team starts using QF2Odoo or the Odoo UNS Connector, agree what a successful transfer should look like. Use an agreed test example and the workflow enabled for your company.

[Nederlands](/nl/quickstart)

## Before you begin

The documented integrations target **Odoo 19**. Your administrator needs to confirm the installed release, relevant Odoo apps, access rights and enabled exchanges. QuotationFactory is needed for QF2Odoo; a configured UNS connection and receiving systems are needed for the Odoo UNS Connector.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Agree where each change belongs">
    Identify who maintains customer and article data, who confirms quotations, and which system reports each production operation. Name the person responsible for resolving transfer errors.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow one example">
    For QF2Odoo, follow an exported project into its Odoo draft quotation. For the Odoo UNS Connector, follow a manufacturing order snapshot and, where enabled, one authorized feedback message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the business result">
    Compare the source reference, quantities and expected destination record. A sent message or receipt alone does not prove that a quotation, time entry or stock booking is correct.
  </Step>
</Steps>
